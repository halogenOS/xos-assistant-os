its history thrown away twenty lines at a time. The kept-set plan is gone
whole: nothing is discarded now, the older half comes back as prose.

Two arms over the context window arm the unattended trigger, read from
the last turn's reported usage. Headroom: 50k tokens or less left in the
window, close enough to the wall that compacting is overdue whatever else
holds. A cold cache over a half-full window: the prompt cache has expired
and more than half the window is in use, so the next turn pays full price
for the whole prefix anyway and paying it once for a shorter prefix is
the cheap move. Either number unknown keeps both arms silent.

An armed threshold waits for a quiet moment, one rule whichever arm armed
it, tracking the current cache state rather than the state that armed the
trigger. A warm cache turns its own edge into the deadline - the
dispatch goes a margin before expiry so it lands on the warm prefix - and
an already-cold cache has no warm window left to protect, so waiting for
quiet costs nothing. Every dispatch re-warms the cache and restarts the
rule.

One mechanism sits behind three doors: /compact, the five-refusal forced
end, and the threshold. The summary is captured in a temporary fork of
the first half with no tools admitted, so nothing the capture reads can
act. The new thread opens with the ancestor reference, then the digest,
then the second half verbatim, and takes the channel from the old one.
